01 / About

From laboratory-based measurement to field-based biomechanical assessment.

Across three degrees, my work has moved from measuring human movement in the laboratory to making that measurement usable in real-world settings.

Undergraduate and master’s degrees in sport human science at Beijing Sport University in China built a foundation in exercise physiology and sports biomechanics, including laboratory studies of running economy and lower-limb biomechanics in footwear testing. Doctoral research at the University of Auckland applies these methods to occupational ergonomics, addressing two stages of ergonomic design: predicting working posture before a workstation exists, and assessing postural and biomechanical exposure once work is under way.

The work spans laboratory-based biomechanical experiments, video-based human movement analysis, validation studies and software development. The aim is to make validated biomechanical assessment usable outside the laboratory, in support of safer workplace and product design.

Journal articles

2027 Concurrent validity of markerless motion capture for occupational lifting: Kinematics and ergonomic risk assessmentApplied Ergonomics, 138, 104866First authorOpen access View study details

Zhang, M., Nieuwenhuys, A., Boocock, M.*, & Zhang, Y.* · DOI

The question

Can markerless motion capture replace laboratory marker-based systems for ergonomic assessment of occupational lifting, and where does its accuracy break down?

What was done

Twenty healthy adults performed three two-handed lifting tasks of increasing complexity: symmetrical vertical lifting, transverse lifting with trunk rotation, and rotational walking-and-lifting. Kinematics were recorded simultaneously by a multi-view system (OpenCap), a single-camera pipeline (MediaPipe) and a Vicon marker-based reference, and compared by joint, movement plane and REBA risk classification.

Key findings
  • Error increased by 15–30% from symmetrical to asymmetric multi-planar lifting tasks.
  • Both systems showed lower error in the sagittal plane (RMSE 9.9–31.2°) than in the frontal and transverse planes.
  • Both were reliable for symmetrical lifting, with high REBA agreement; validity declined for tasks involving trunk rotation and walking.
  • MediaPipe showed lower error for the trunk and proximal joints, and OpenCap for distal joints, informing practical system selection.
Why it matters for practice

Markerless systems are suitable for preliminary screening of symmetrical lifting, while asymmetric tasks with trunk rotation or walking still call for caution or laboratory-grade measurement. These boundaries informed the design of the online assessment system.

2025 Posture prediction models in digital human modeling for ergonomic design: A systematic reviewMedical Engineering & Physics, 143, 104391First authorOpen access View study details

Zhang, M., Nieuwenhuys, A., & Zhang, Y.* · DOI

The question

How are posture prediction models in digital human modelling developed and validated, and how ready are they for ergonomic design practice?

What was done

A PRISMA-guided systematic review across nine academic databases, including 24 studies: 12 data-driven models and 12 optimisation-based models.

Key findings
  • Data-driven models, such as neural network regression, showed strong predictive accuracy and adaptability, but limited generalisability owing to data imbalance and limited participant and task diversity.
  • Optimisation-based models, such as gradient-descent and genetic-algorithm approaches, offered high biomechanical fidelity but faced computational challenges and limited CAD integration.
  • Few models were integrated with CAD software such as JACK and Santos; most lacked ergonomic evaluation and real-time usability.
Why it matters for practice

The review identifies the need for scalable motion data from computer-vision technologies and for hybrid strategies that combine learning-based inference with biomechanical simulation, the direction taken in the hybrid posture prediction framework.

A Hybrid Two-Phase Framework for Posture Prediction of Manual Material Handling Tasks OralZhang, M., Boocock, M., Nieuwenhuys, A., & Zhang, Y.10th World Congress of Biomechanics (WCB 2026), Vancouver, Canada · Session OA174 Occupational Biomechanics Technology · 15 July 2026A fast whole-body posture prediction method for design-stage ergonomic evaluation.
View details
Purpose
Predicts how a worker is likely to stand, bend and reach for a given task (hand and foot positions, external load and body size) before a workstation, tool or product exists.
Approach
Constrained sagittal-plane optimisation with five degrees of freedom determines leg and trunk posture; the four-degree-of-freedom 3D arm is then solved analytically in closed form, removing the iterative search that makes full 3D optimisation slow.
Validation
Compared with marker-based motion capture in 12 participants across vertical lifting, shoulder-height drilling and above-shoulder drilling. Joint-angle RMSE ranged from 4.0° to 37.5°; trunk and shoulder flexion errors (≤10°) fell below the 10–20° intervals used in observational risk assessment, with the largest error in humeral axial rotation during lifting. Total computation time was approximately 85 ms per posture on a standard laptop.
Applications
Real-time what-if analysis in CAD and digital human modelling platforms such as JACK and RAMSIS, for example adjusting drilling height or reach distance and seeing the postural consequence immediately; early-stage workstation and tool design without physical prototypes; and posture-aware planning in human–robot collaboration, where about 12 updates per second exceeds the 0.5–2 Hz typically required.

2027 Concurrent validity of markerless motion capture for occupational lifting: Kinematics and ergonomic risk assessmentApplied Ergonomics, 138, 104866第一作者开放获取 查看研究详情

Zhang, M., Nieuwenhuys, A., Boocock, M.*, & Zhang, Y.* · DOI

研究问题

无标记动作捕捉能否替代实验室标记点系统用于职业搬举的工效学评估？其准确度在什么情况下下降？

研究方法

20 名健康成年人完成三类复杂度递增的双手搬举任务：对称垂直搬举、伴躯干旋转的横向搬举，以及旋转行走搬举。多视角系统（OpenCap）、单摄像头方案（MediaPipe）与 Vicon 标记点参考系统同步记录运动学数据，并按关节、运动平面与 REBA 风险分级进行比较。

主要发现
  • 由对称搬举到非对称多平面搬举，误差增加 15–30%。
  • 两套系统在矢状面的误差（RMSE 9.9–31.2°）均低于冠状面与水平面。
  • 两者在对称搬举中可靠，REBA 一致性高；在涉及躯干旋转与行走的任务中效度下降。
  • MediaPipe 在躯干与近端关节误差较低，OpenCap 在远端关节表现更好，可为系统选型提供依据。
实践意义

无标记系统适用于对称搬举的初步筛查；涉及躯干旋转或行走的非对称任务仍需谨慎，或采用实验室级测量。这些适用边界已用于在线评估系统的设计。

研究问题

数字人体建模中的姿态预测模型如何开发与验证？距离工效学设计实践还有多远？

研究方法

依据 PRISMA 指南检索九个学术数据库，纳入 24 项研究：数据驱动模型与优化模型各 12 项。

主要发现
  • 数据驱动模型（如神经网络回归）预测精度高、适应性强，但受数据不平衡及受试者与任务多样性不足影响，泛化能力有限。
  • 优化模型（如梯度下降与遗传算法）生物力学保真度高，但面临计算效率挑战，与 CAD 的整合有限。
  • 少数模型已与 JACK、Santos 等 CAD 软件整合，但多数缺少工效学评价与实时可用性。
实践意义

综述指出，需要借助计算机视觉获取可扩展的运动数据，并发展结合学习推断与生物力学仿真的混合策略——这也是混合姿态预测框架所采用的方向。

查看详情
用途
在工位、工具或产品实际建成之前，根据任务条件（手足位置、外部负荷与体型）预测工人可能采取的站姿、弯腰与伸手姿态。
方法
先以 5 个自由度的矢状面约束优化确定下肢与躯干姿态，再以解析方法闭式求解 4 个自由度的三维上肢构型，省去了全三维优化耗时的迭代搜索。
验证
在 12 名受试者的垂直搬举、肩高钻孔与过肩钻孔任务中，与标记点动作捕捉比较：关节角度 RMSE 为 4.0°–37.5°；躯干与肩关节屈伸误差（≤10°）低于观察法风险评估 10–20° 的分级区间，最大误差出现在搬举时的肱骨轴向旋转。在普通笔记本电脑上，单个姿态总计算时间约 85 ms。
应用
可在 JACK、RAMSIS 等 CAD 与数字人体建模平台中进行实时假设分析，例如调整钻孔高度或伸手距离后立即查看姿态变化；支持无需实物原型的早期工位与工具设计；也可用于人机协作中的姿态感知规划，约每秒 12 次的更新频率高于通常所需的 0.5–2 Hz。
